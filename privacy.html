BODYBOX Privacy Policy

Team BodyBox · BODYBOX Programming · Last updated November 17, 2026

What the BODYBOX app is

The BODYBOX app is the training app for Team BodyBox athletes. It shows your programming, keeps your logbook and lets you record workout videos with the BODYBOX clock on screen.

What we keep

  • Your account email and name, so you can sign in and so your coach can approve you.
  • What you log: lifts, scores, notes, check-ins and links to videos you choose to share.

This is stored in Google Firebase and is only seen by you and your Team BodyBox coaches. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Videos

Videos you record stay on your phone unless you save or post them. Recording uses your camera and microphone only while the recorder is open.

Posting to YouTube

If you choose "Post to YouTube", you sign in with your own Google account and the app asks for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and to read your channel name. The app only uses that permission to upload the video you choose, with the title and privacy setting you pick. Your Google password never reaches us, and we don't keep your Google access token on our servers. You can unlink at any time in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own terms apply to anything you post: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Email teambodybox@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and everything you logged.
