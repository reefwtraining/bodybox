Step 2

See what a day looks like

Every program follows the same rhythm: warm up with purpose, build strength or skill, then a workout that tests it. These example days show the style of each program and what each part is for.

Rebuilt Training Class · Example day

60 min · all levels
Warm-up
3 min easy row, then 2 rounds: 10 Air Squats, 10 Ring Rows, 5 Inchworms, 3 Broad Jumps

Moves every joint you'll use today and adds a little explosive work to wake up the legs.

Strength
Deadlift · 5 sets of 3, building to a heavy set

One main lift a day. You pick the weight, and coaches help you find a heavy but clean set.

Workout
12 min AMRAP
200 m Row
12 Wall Balls 20/14 lb
10 Russian KB Swings 53/35 lb RX+ 70/53

A conditioning piece that keeps you moving. Every movement has a scaled option, so beginners and veterans do the same workout.

Saturdays are longer workouts done solo or with a partner: machines, carries, sled or sandbag work and plenty of teamwork.

Athlete Comp · Example day

done on top of class
Warm-up
3 min bike, then 2–3 sets: 8 KB RDL, 8 Goblet Squats, 5/5 Single-Arm Press
Explosive primer: 3 sets of 3 Seated Box Jumps + 3 Clean Pulls

Gets the hips and shoulders ready, then trains speed before you touch a heavy bar.

Strength
Power Clean + Front Squat complex
5 sets: 3+1 @ 60% · 2+1 @ 70% · 1+1 @ 75 / 80 / 80%

Percentages are of your best lift, so the day fits your level. Pairing the clean with a squat builds strength in the catch.

Workout
Every 3:00 × 5 rounds
12/10 Cal Row
8 Toes-to-Bar
6 Thrusters 95/65 lb RX+ 115/80
Rest the remaining time

Repeatable hard efforts with rest, just like heats at a competition. The goal is holding the same pace every round.

BODYBOX Building · Example day

lower body
Warm-up
4 min bike for distance, then 3 rounds: 10 Glute Bridges, 10 Bodyweight Lunges, 20 Jumping Jacks

Raises the heart rate and gets blood into the muscles you're about to train.

Strength
Back Squat 5 × 5 @ 70%
Romanian Deadlift 4 × 8
DB Walking Lunge 3 × 20 steps
Leg Extension or Banded Knee Extension 3 × 15

Classic muscle-building work: one heavy lift for strength, then higher-rep accessories for size. Technique notes come with every movement.

Core
3 sets, 1 min rest: :30 Weighted Plank · 12 Hanging Knee Raises · 10/10 Side Plank Reach-Throughs

Finishes with midline work that carries over to every lift.

Cardio · Example day

run day
Warm-up
10 reps each: leg swings, lateral lunges, hip openers, down-dog calf stretch, high knees, butt kickers

A runner's dynamic warm-up that opens the hips and ankles before faster work.

Workout
6 × 400 m at goal 5K pace
90 sec walk between

Teaches your body what race pace feels like, with enough rest to hit it every time.

Cool-down
2 sets: 15 Tibialis Raises · 10 Banded Chinese Squats

Light accessory work to keep shins and knees healthy. No heavy weight.

Other days use the rower, ski erg and bikes, with real technique coaching: row "1 o'clock to 11 o'clock," ski "cheek to cheek," and drive the heel down on the bike.

Garage Box · Example day

four versions · pick yours
Warm-up
3 min jump rope or jog in place, then 2 rounds: 10 Air Squats, 10 Backpack Good Mornings, 8 Push-ups, 3 Broad Jumps

Gets the hips and shoulders moving with whatever you have at home.

Strength
Front Squat · 5 × 5, build across the sets
RX · Equipment Double DB Front Squat, 3-sec lower
Scaled · Equipment Goblet Squat, 2-sec pause
RX · No equipment Backpack hugged at your chest, 2-sec pause
Scaled · No equipment Air Squat to a chair

No barbell, so tempo and pauses make light loads feel heavy. Each set should feel a little harder than the last.

Workout
12 min AMRAP
RX · Equipment 300 m Run · 12 DB Thrusters 2×35/2×20 · 10 Burpee DB Jump-Overs
Scaled · Equipment 200 m Run · 12 Single DB Thrusters 35/20 · 8 Burpee DB Step-Overs
RX · No equipment 300 m Run · 12 Backpack Thrusters · 10 Burpee Backpack Jump-Overs
Scaled · No equipment 30 Jumping Jacks + 10 Mountain Climbers · 12 Air Squats with an overhead reach · 8 Step-back Burpees

The same intent in every version: a steady engine with legs and lungs working together. Pick the version that matches your gear and keeps you moving.

The backpack is the dumbbell for the no-equipment versions. Load it with books, water bottles or a bag of rice, pack it tight, and you're ready. Garage Box comes with its own scaling guide, including run conversions for when there's no room to run.

S.H.I.I.T · Example day

Mon · Wed · Fri
Format
Timed blocks, each with one focus: warm-up, power, conditioning, finisher

The clock does the coaching. Work hard for the interval, rest, and go again.

Workout
:40 work / :20 rest × 3 rounds
Squat Jumps · Push-ups · DB or KB Swings · Mountain Climbers · Reverse Lunges

Bodyweight and plyometrics with at most one dumbbell or kettlebell, so it works at home or in a hotel room.

The goal is moving with efficiency. Adjust the weight; there's no need to go heavy.

How to read the board

EMOM
Every minute on the minute. Start a set at the top of each minute; whatever time is left is rest.
AMRAP
As many rounds or reps as possible in the time given.
For time
Finish the work as fast as you can, with good form.
75/55
Suggested weight in pounds. First number for men, second for women.
RX / RX+
RX is the workout as written. RX+ is a harder version. Scaled options are always available.
@ 70%
Use 70% of your best single lift for that movement.
4-6-4-6
Reps per set, in order. Each number is one set.
5RM
Rep max. The heaviest weight you can lift for 5 good reps.
Where it started · Fall 2020

BodyBoxThrowdown 2020

When gyms were closed and competitions were cancelled, we built our own. The BodyBox Throwdown was a free, four-week online competition that brought 120 athletes together on one leaderboard, competing from CrossFit boxes and garage gyms in nine countries.

  • Germany
  • England

  • France

Divisions

RX · Scaled · Garage Athlete · Garage Athlete Scaled. The Garage divisions were built for people training at home with limited equipment, the same way BODYBOX started.

  1. Week 1

    Workout 20.1

    The opener dropped with movement standard videos so everyone knew exactly what counted.

  2. Week 2

    20.2 + 20.2A

    A two-part week that tested both engine and skill.

  3. Week 3

    Workout 20.3

    Athletes filmed their efforts and sent in video for judging.

  4. Week 4

    Workout 20.4

    The finale, scored, judged and posted to the final leaderboard.

Watch the promoThrowdown 2020 promo video on YouTube ↗

Every score was backed by video. We partnered with WODProof for time-stamped competition timers, gave away weekly prizes with Game Face Wipes, and ran it all on Competition Corner. When it was over, we sat down and recorded a review of the whats and whys, because the athletes' feedback shapes how we build everything, including the programming on this page.

Before you start

Questions

Do I need a gym?

It depends on the program. S.H.I.I.T needs floor space and one dumbbell or kettlebell, so it works at home or in a hotel. Garage Box works with dumbbells or kettlebells and a jump rope, or with no equipment at all: a loaded backpack, a chair and a wall. Cardio needs a road, track or machine. Athlete Comp and BODYBOX Building need a gym with a barbell and plates.

I'm a beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Every workout comes with scaling options, so you can lower the weight, the reps or swap a movement. Rebuilt Training Class is coached in person and built for all levels.

How do I get the workouts?

In the BODYBOX app. After you pay, we send you a program code and your sign-in. Enter the code and your training shows up every day, with the timer, your weights and movement demos.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Your first 7 days of any program are free. Email teambodybox@gmail.com with the program you want to try.

Can I do more than one program?

Yes. Stack any programs you want, or get all five with the Full BODYBOX Bundle for $129 a month, personalized to you every 4 weeks.

Can I switch programs?

Yes. Email teambodybox@gmail.com and tell us which program you want to switch to.

Can I cancel?

Plans are paid month to month. If you don't want another month, just don't pay for the next one, or email us.

What's the difference between Rebuilt Training Class and the online programs?

Rebuilt Training Class is the in-person group class at Rebuilt Training in Kenilworth, NJ, booked through StreamFit. The online programs are BODYBOX programming you follow on your own, anywhere, in the app.

Is there a team chat?

Yes. Join the TEAMBODYBOX group on GroupMe to talk training, ask questions and get updates: join the team chat ↗

I'm a Rebuilt Training member. Do I get a deal?

Yes. Rebuilt members pay $25 a month per program, or $119 a month for the full bundle.
